## Runs from your Galaxy Watch

Run with your Galaxy Watch, and the run appears in FITOS by itself:

1. Your watch records the run in Samsung Health.
2. Samsung Health shares it with **Health Connect**.
3. Next time you open **Running**, FITOS brings it in, marked with a watch icon.

Each watch run shows your **average and maximum heart rate**. When Samsung Health shares the route, you also get the map, splits, best efforts and replay. When it doesn't, the run keeps your watch's distance and time.

A run you recorded on the phone as well is not doubled, and a run never comes in twice, even after a reinstall.

**To turn it on:** Running → settings → **Import runs from Galaxy Watch**. Health Connect asks you to allow exercise, distance, heart rate and routes.

## Fifteen new badges

- **Speed:** 5K under 30, 25 and 20 minutes, 10K under 60 and 50 minutes, half marathon under 2 hours, marathon under 4 hours.
- **Lifetime distance:** 50, 100, 250, 500 and 1,000 km run in total.
- **Consistency:** four weeks in a row, three runs in a week, and finishing a running plan.

Your past runs count, so you may already have some. **Streak & rewards** shows how close you are to each one: your best time, or how many kilometres to go.

## Two new rewards

- **1,000 km** in total unlocks the **Mileage** theme.
- A **5K under 20 minutes** unlocks the **Bolt** app icon.

## Also

The streak page now shows the date on every day of the last four weeks.

## Your privacy

Health Connect data stays on your phone, with one exception, and only if you switch it on: your watch runs, including their heart rate, are kept with your account like any run. They're in Download my data, deleted with your account, and never sent to any AI. The privacy policy now says this, and you'll be asked to accept the new version once.
