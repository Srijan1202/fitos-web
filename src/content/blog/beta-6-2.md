## Five boards

Every board can now rank by **gym sessions**, **run km**, **streak**, **active days** or **kept my plan**. "Kept my plan" counts the sessions you did out of those your plan had up to today, so someone training 3 days a week can beat someone training 6. People without a plan are not on that board.

## Last week

Above each board you now see last week's top three and your place, with how many places you moved since the week before: "You were 3rd last week · up 2".

## Your friends' week

Tap a friend, or someone in your club, to see their week. It shows their streak, this week's sessions, run distance and active days, how much of their plan they kept, and the 4 weeks before. You also see the clubs you share. It holds only counts: never weights, food, body measurements or where anyone ran.

## Clubs you run

If you made a club, ⋯ → **Club settings** lets you:

- rename the club;
- make a new code, so the old one stops working;
- remove a member, who can't join again with the code until you let them back;
- pass the club to someone else.

## Invite links and QR codes

**Share** on a club now sends a link like tryfitos.me/c/ABCD2345. Your handle has a QR code too: Community settings → **My QR code**, or the QR button beside "Invite a friend". A friend points their phone camera at it, and FITOS opens on "Add @you?". If they don't have FITOS yet, the link shows them how to get it.

## Safer and quicker

- After someone declines your friend request, you can't send them another for 7 days.
- Your counts update as soon as you finish a session or save a run.
- tryfitos.me and the FITOS server now send stricter browser security headers.

## Privacy

The privacy policy (version 2026-11-11) now covers two things:

- **The new counts.** Community shows active days, how much of your plan you kept, and the last 4 weeks of your counts, to the same people who already saw your week.
- **Database snapshots.** A snapshot of the database is taken before each server update, and each one is deleted within 30 days.

The app asks you to accept the new policy once.

## Getting it

This is a new version, 1.0.0-beta.6.2. The app will ask you to update, or you can download it from this site. It installs over the version you have and keeps everything.
