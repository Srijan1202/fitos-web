Beta 5.1 fixes what you found in 5.0's demos and widgets.

## Clearer demos

- **Arms show in front of the body.** Every part of the figure has a thin outline, so a curl or a pulldown no longer disappears into the torso. The figure is also bigger in its frame.
- **Bench press:** the bar moves in an arc, from over your shoulders down to your lower chest.
- **Deadlift:** your knees move back as the bar passes them. The bar stays in front of your legs the whole way.
- **Romanian deadlift:** comes back up the way it went down.
- **Overhead press:** the bar goes in front of your face as your head moves back, then your head moves through once the bar is overhead.
- **Overhead triceps extension:** the dumbbell drops behind your head.
- **Goblet squat:** one dumbbell, held upright at your chest.
- **Cable fly:** elbows stay soft and out, like hugging a barrel.
- **Lat pulldown:** now shown from the front, seated, with the wide bar coming down to your chest.
- **Rear delt fly:** easier to see you're bent over.
- **Hammer curl:** moves like the curl, with a note to keep your palms facing each other.
- **Single-arm dumbbell row:** the arm no longer swings up over your back.

## Widgets

- **A tap opens FITOS on the right screen.** In 5.0 it showed "Page Not Found".
- **Quick actions and Streak show their icons** again (🍽️ 🏋️ 🏃 🔥).
- **The widget list shows a preview** of each widget with sample figures.

## Getting it

Open FITOS and tap **Update now**, or [download beta 5.1](/download/).
