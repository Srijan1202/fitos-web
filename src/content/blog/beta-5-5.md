Logging food is quicker now: point your camera at your plate.

## How it works

1. Open **Log food** and tap **Snap a meal**.
2. Take a photo, or pick one from your gallery.
3. FITOS AI lists each food it can see, with an amount and its calories and protein.
4. Check it, fix an amount if it's off, untick anything that isn't yours, and tap **Add**.

Nothing is logged until you tap Add.

## Mess plates

If you've chosen your mess in Profile, FITOS AI reads the photo against that meal's menu. Dishes it finds on the menu use the mess's own numbers and are logged as mess dishes, just like tapping them on the Mess tab.

## Good to know

- **Amounts are a guess.** A photo shows what's on the plate better than how much. Use + and − to fix a portion before you add it.
- **Your photo is never kept.** It goes to FITOS AI for that one reading, then it's deleted from your phone and never stored on our server.
- **Snap a meal is part of FITOS Premium**, like the other FITOS AI features. You can send up to 20 photos a day.

The privacy policy now says what a meal photo sends. FITOS asks you to agree once more the next time you open it.

## Getting it

The update comes to the app by itself. Close FITOS and open it again; there's nothing to download.
