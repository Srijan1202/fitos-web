## Every reward, and when it unlocks

**Profile → Rewards** now shows everything you can earn in one place:

- **Streak rewards:** the Spark picture, the Ember theme, the Blaze app icon, the Ember rim and the Century badge. Each shows the streak that unlocks it and how many days you are from it, with a progress bar.
- **Running badges:** first run, 5K, 10K, half marathon and marathon. Each shows what it unlocks (the Finisher icon, the Road theme, the gold 42 icon) and how far your longest run is from it.
- **Premium:** the app icons, themes, pictures and the AI coach that come with Premium.

At the top, it counts how many you've earned. Anything you've earned stays yours for good.

## FITOS has its own signing key

Until now, FITOS was signed with a temporary developer key, which makes Android's Play Protect extra cautious. From this version, FITOS is signed with its own release key.

You don't need to do anything: this update installs over your current FITOS like any other and keeps all your data. Play Protect may still check FITOS because it isn't from the Play Store, but it no longer flags a temporary key.
