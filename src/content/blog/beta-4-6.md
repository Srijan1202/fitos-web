## A bolder Gym / Running switch

The switch at the top of Training is now bigger and easier to hit. The side you're on is a solid block that slides across when you switch.

## Every tab looks the same at the top

Every tab now starts the same way: a small label, a big title, and the page's button on the right.

- **Running:** "Your runs", with settings on the right.
- **Gym:** your programme's name, with its ⋯ menu.
- **AI:** "FITOS AI", with New chat.
- **Nutrition:** the day as the title, with ‹ › to move between days, and Food library on the right.

## No more black bars

Pages you open, such as a run, your records, Profile or the mess menu, used to have a solid black bar at the top. That bar is gone, and the page's green glow now runs all the way to the top.

## A tidier Running page

- **Choose a plan** is a proper button now.
- **Records** shows each time on the right, with the date under the distance.
- The kilometres / miles switch matches the app's other switches.

## Runs no longer look unsynced

After you saved a run, its page could keep saying "Not synced yet" until you closed FITOS, even though the run had already reached FITOS. Now that note goes away about a second after the run is saved.

## Paper theme retired

The Paper theme is gone. If you were using it, FITOS now opens on FITOS Light.

## Getting it

Open FITOS and tap **Update now**. Or [download beta 4.6](/download/).
