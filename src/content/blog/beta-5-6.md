## Snap a meal from your gallery

Snap a meal still opens the camera first. If the photo you want is already on your phone, tap **Gallery** next to the shutter and pick it, with no need to take a new photo first.

## Exercise demos, redrawn

We went through the exercise demos one by one and redrew 62 of them:

- Machines now show their arms and handles.
- Lowering is slower and controlled.
- Several lifts are shown from a clearer angle. The flies and the floor press are seen from above.

Each redrawn demo appears in the app as soon as it has been checked.

## Getting it

The update comes to the app by itself. Close FITOS and open it again; there's nothing to download.
