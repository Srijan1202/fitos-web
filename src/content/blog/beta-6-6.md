## The club league

Enter your club in the FITOS league and it plays a club of a similar level every week, with no codes to swap. Every Saturday the league pairs the clubs for the coming week by active days per member, so gym-goers and runners count the same, and a small club can beat a big one.

A win is 3 points and a draw 1. Seasons last four weeks. The club page shows your place, the top of the table and your next opponent, and at the end of a season everyone gets one notification with the club's place. Last season's winner gets a trophy by its name.

Any club with 3 or more members can enter. Other clubs in the league see your club's name, results and average, never who is in it.

## Co-owners

A club's owner can now name up to two co-owners. They can set the month's goal, take on other clubs, enter the league and look after members, so the club keeps going when the owner is busy. If the owner leaves, a co-owner takes over.

## Share a result

Won a match, or climbing the table? Tap **Share** to send a picture of the result with your club's name and average. It's made on your phone, and no one's name is on it.

## Your club in numbers

The top of every club now shows the month so far: sessions, run kilometres and active days, all of you together, and the club's best week.
