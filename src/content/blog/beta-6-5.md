## A feed in every club

Open a club and switch between **Board** and **Feed**. The feed shows what members share: sessions they finish, runs, records and streak milestones, from after they joined the club. Cheer anyone's post, just like a friend's.

Sharing is now on from the moment you join Community. Your friends and your clubs see the same things. Turn off anything you'd rather keep to yourself in Community settings, and its posts go with it. Weights, food, body measurements and where you ran are never shared.

## A goal for the month

A club's owner can set a challenge for the month: a number of sessions, run kilometres or active days to reach together. FITOS suggests a target from the club's last four weeks. Everyone sees the bar fill up, how many days are left and their own part. When the club gets there, everyone gets one notification. The last three challenges stay on the club, reached or not.

## Club vs club

Owners can challenge another club, by its code, to a week: Monday to Sunday, sessions, run km or active days. The score is the average per member, so a small club can beat a big one. The other club only ever sees your club's name and average, never who is in it. When the week is over, everyone gets one notification with the result.

## Before you start

The privacy policy has a new version that covers all of this. You'll be asked to agree to it once. If you were already in Community, sharing switches on when you agree, and you can turn any of it off again in Community settings.
