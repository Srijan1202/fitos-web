Beta 3.8 is all from your feedback. Thank you for every message.

## Training

- **Your set weights stay put.** Editing a day in **Edit this day** no longer hides last time's weights until you reopen the app.
- **High-to-low cable fly** is in the exercise library, as a swap for the cable fly and the other flies.
- **Discard a workout you started by mistake.** Every **Resume** now has **Discard** beside it, on Home, Training and TODAY. If nothing was logged, the session is removed completely. If you logged sets, it stays in your history as discarded.

## Streaks

**A rest day now counts toward your streak by itself.** Rest is part of training, so there is nothing to log. Home tells you, and FITOS no longer warns you that your streak is at risk on a rest day. A rest day can't start a streak on its own, though: it adds to one that's already going.

## Logging weight

**Log weight** on Home is now a quick popup: type today's weight and save. You can also add your waist, chest, arm, thigh or hip measurements in the same popup, if you want to. Your targets update from your weight as before.

## A camera inside FITOS

Progress photos and feedback pictures are now taken with a camera inside FITOS, so Android no longer closes the app while your phone's camera is open. The first time, Android asks to let FITOS use the camera. Choosing a photo from the gallery works as before.

## FITOS AI writes as it thinks

FITOS AI answers now appear word by word as they are written, instead of all at once after a wait.
