Beta 5.0 brought animated demos for 30 lifts. In 5.2, every exercise in the library has one: 160 demos in all, plus the close variants that share them (the chin-up uses the pull-up's demo, with a note on the grip).

## Checked before you see them

Each demo is checked before it appears in the app. The 29 you have already seen are ready now. The new ones will appear over the next few days, as they're approved. You don't need to update again: FITOS picks them up the next time you open it.

Until a demo is ready, the exercise still shows its written steps, as before.

## Where to find them

- On an exercise's page, above "How to do it".
- During a workout, from the play icon beside the exercise's name.
- All together in Profile → About → **Exercise demos**.

## The cable fly

The cable fly is drawn again, from the front. Your arms stay long and soft and sweep toward you and across your chest, instead of bending at the elbow.

## Something look wrong?

If a demo doesn't match how you do the lift, tell us through Feedback in the app. Say which exercise it is and which part looks off.
