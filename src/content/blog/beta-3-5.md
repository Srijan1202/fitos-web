Beta 3.5 is a fixes release, built from what you told us.

## Meal reminders that learn when you eat

FITOS can now remind you to log breakfast, lunch and dinner, and it times each one around you:

- **At first**, at 9:00, 14:00 and 21:00, while FITOS learns your routine.
- **After a week of logging**, about half an hour after you usually log that meal, and it keeps adjusting as your days change.
- **A meal you usually skip** gets no reminder.
- **Already logged?** No reminder. Logging lunch only cancels the lunch reminder.

Turn each one on or off in **Profile → Notifications**. FITOS now sends at most four reminders a day, at least two hours apart.

## No workout reminder once you've trained

If you started a workout that day, the workout reminder and the missed-session nudge no longer come, even if your session hasn't synced yet.

## You won't miss an update

- When a new beta is out, FITOS tells you **every time you open it**, over whatever screen you're on (never in the middle of a workout).
- The update banner sits at the top of Home.
- You'll also get a notification when a new version is out.

## Feedback

You can now send up to 20 messages an hour from **Profile → Send feedback**, and if you hit the limit FITOS tells you kindly instead of showing an error.
