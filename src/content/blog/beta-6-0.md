## One tap a glass

Home has a new **Water** card under Today. Tap **+ Glass** to add 250 ml, and **−** to take one off. The bar fills as you go, and the card tells you how many glasses are left.

For other amounts, tap the card: +500 ml, +750 ml or +1 L in one go.

## Your target

Your target is 35 ml for each kg of your latest weigh-in, rounded to the nearest glass and kept between 1.5 and 4 litres. Until you log a weigh-in it is 2.5 litres. It's a guide: heat, sweat and the food you eat all change how much you need.

## Works offline

Taps count straight away, even without a connection. FITOS keeps the day's total on your phone and sends it when you're back online. Until then, the card says "not synced yet".

## Privacy

The privacy policy now lists the water you drink each day (version 2026-11-08.2), and the app asks you to accept it once. Your water is in Download my data and is deleted with your account.

## Getting it

This is a new version, 1.0.0-beta.6.0. FITOS will offer **Update now**, or you can download it from this site. It installs over the version you have and keeps everything.
