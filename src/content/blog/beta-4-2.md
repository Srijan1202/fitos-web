Beta 4.2 adds your running records. It looks for your fastest stretch of each distance inside every run, not only runs of exactly that length.

## Your records

FITOS keeps your best time for seven distances: **400 m, 1 km, 1 mile, 5 km, 10 km, half marathon and marathon.**

- **Inside any run.** A 10 km run also gives you a 1 km, a mile and a 5 km: the fastest stretch of each, wherever it was in the run.
- **Pauses don't count.** Stopping at a crossing (auto-pause) doesn't slow your record, the same as your splits.
- **Every run you've already saved counts.** Open **Records** on Running and FITOS works out your old runs too.
- **Your best three** at each distance: tap a distance to see them, and tap one to open that run.

A run's page now has a **Best efforts** section: each distance the run covers, with its time and pace, and a gold, silver or bronze medal when it's one of your best three. In your history, runs that hold a record have a trophy.

## "New record!"

When you finish a run that beats a record, the summary says so straight away, even offline: "New record: 5 km in 24:31". If the voice coach is on, it tells you too.

Phone GPS is accurate to a few metres, so a 400 m time can be a second or so out. Longer distances are more accurate.

## Badges

Five distance badges, each earned with one run of at least that distance. Treadmill runs count.

| Badge | Unlocks |
|---|---|
| First run | — |
| First 5K | — |
| First 10K | the **Finisher** app icon |
| First half marathon | the **Road** theme |
| First marathon | the gold **42** app icon |

Your past runs count, so you may already have some. See them under **Running → Badges**, and choose an unlocked icon or theme in **Profile**.

## Your privacy

Records and badges are worked out from runs FITOS already has. Nothing new is collected. They're in **Download my data**, and they're deleted with your account.

## Getting it

Open FITOS and tap **Update now** when it asks. It installs over 4.1.1 and keeps all your data. Or [download beta 4.2](/download/).
