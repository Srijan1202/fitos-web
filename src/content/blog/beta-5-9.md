## Community, one tap away

Community now has its own tab at the end of the bottom bar, with the trophy. Your friends, your clubs and the world board are always one tap away.

## Food, Progress and Recovery together

Nutrition now has a switch at the top, the same one Training uses for Gym and Running:

- **Food:** your day's meals against your targets, as before. Nutrition opens here.
- **Progress:** your weight trend, strength, training, nutrition and body.
- **Recovery:** today's check-in and readiness, sleep and heart rate.

Everything on those pages is the same; they just live together now. The shortcuts still take you straight there. On Home, readiness and sleep open Recovery, and so does the check-in reminder. The weigh-in and the weekly recap open Progress.

## Getting it

This is a new version, 1.0.0-beta.5.9. FITOS will offer **Update now**, or you can download it from this site. It installs over the version you have and keeps everything.
