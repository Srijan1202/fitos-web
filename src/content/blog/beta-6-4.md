## Rest timer on the lock screen

Lock your phone between sets and the rest keeps counting on the lock screen. Tap **+15 s** for a little longer, or **Skip** when you're ready, without unlocking FITOS. When the rest is up you still get "Rest over". The countdown only shows while FITOS is in the background; in the app, the rest bar does the job.

## Which plates go on the bar

On any barbell exercise, the new plates button by the exercise's name shows what goes on each side for the weight of your next set, drawn on the bar. Use − and + to try other weights. If a weight can't be made with your plates, it tells you the nearest ones that can.

Every gym is different, so set yours in Profile → **Bar and plates**: a 10, 15, 20 or 25 kg bar, and the plate sizes your gym has.

## Widget buttons

The Actions widget on your home screen has two new buttons:
- **Water** adds a 250 ml glass without opening FITOS, and says your total for the day. FITOS adds it to your day the next time you open the app.
- **Session** opens FITOS straight into today's workout, already started. If a session is running, it opens that one; on a rest day, it opens your plan.
