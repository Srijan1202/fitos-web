Beta 4.8 is all fixes and polish: no new features, just what's already there working better.

## Your streak, month by month

**Streak & rewards** now shows a full calendar month, every day from the 1st to the 31st. Tap ‹ › or swipe to go back through earlier months, as far as the month you joined.

**Rest days now show in green**, even on days you logged a meal. They only count as logged if you trained that day. Missed days have an outline, so they don't look like skipped ones.

## See what you're working towards

Each reward now shows what it unlocks: the picture, the theme's colours or the app icon, with a lock on top until you earn it. Running badges show their own badge.

## A weight graph that fits

The weight graph now fits your readings. A half-kilo change no longer looks flat, and the line starts at your first reading instead of sitting in a corner.

## Save is always reachable

Some sheets, like **Log weight**, opened underneath the tab bar and hid their Save button. Every sheet now opens above it.

## Little things

- Home's cards no longer cut their text short, and "this week" fits inside the ring.
- Today's mess and Suggested plate now sit in one card on Nutrition.
- Section labels and spacing line up the same on every page.
- Switches look the same everywhere.

Under the hood, FITOS, its server and its tools are all updated to current versions.

## Getting it

Open FITOS and tap **Update now**. Or [download beta 4.8](/download/).
