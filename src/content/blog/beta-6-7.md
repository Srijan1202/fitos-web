---
title: Beta 6.7 — A new look for Community
date: 2026-11-27
description: Community in three tabs, people in one place, clubs that show what's on, and Progress and Recovery on Home.
---

## Community in three tabs

Community now opens on the **Feed**, with **Boards** and **Clubs** beside it. Your friends' posts sit under Today, Yesterday or the date, with a small mark for a session, a run, a record or a streak.

**People** has its own place, behind the icon at the top, with a badge when someone is waiting. Requests, your friends, people you may know and your invite are all there.

## One board

Pick Friends, your country or the world, then the week, the month or all time and what to compare, all on one line. Last week's top three stand on a real podium, first place in the middle.

## Clubs that show what's on

Every club on your list now shows one line of what's happening: this week's match and the score so far, or the month's goal with a bar. The clubs you run come first.

Open a club and the match and the goal come first. **Invite** shows the code, the QR and a link in one place.

## Progress and Recovery on Home

Home now has **Today · Progress · Recovery** at the top. Today is Home as you know it. Nutrition is just your food again.

## Smaller things

Chosen chips are in your theme's colour on every screen. Training volume sits at the bottom of the This week card, and an empty board or feed points you to people to add.
