## The Feed

Community has a new **Feed**, next to Friends. It shows what your friends did:
- "@asha finished Push"
- "@ravi ran 5.2 km"
- "@meera set a 5K record"
- "@dev hit a 30-day streak"

Nothing of yours is shared until you choose. Community settings → **Share with friends** has a switch for each: sessions you finish, runs, records and streak milestones. Only friends see your posts, only from the moment you turn a kind on, and never your weights, food, body or where you ran. Turning a kind off deletes its posts, and every post goes after 30 days.

## Cheers

Tap the heart on a friend's post to cheer it. On your own posts you see who cheered. You get one evening notification a day, such as "@asha and 2 others cheered you.", never a stream of them.

## Nudge a quiet friend

If a friend hasn't trained for 3 days, their profile has a **Nudge** button. It sends the same short message every time: "time to move. A short session counts." You can nudge each friend once a week, and nobody gets more than one nudge notification a day. You can switch cheer and nudge notifications off in Community settings.

## Month and all time

Every board now has **Week**, **Month** and **All time**. All time counts from the day you joined Community, so newcomers aren't buried under people who joined earlier.

## People you may know

Friends now suggests friends of your friends and people in your clubs. A suggestion shows "2 friends in common" or the club you share, never which friends. Tap ✕ to hide someone for good. To stay out of other people's suggestions, turn off **Suggest me to friends of friends** in Community settings.

## Privacy

The privacy policy (version 2026-11-12) now covers the feed, cheers, nudges, suggestions and the month and all-time counts. FITOS asks you to agree to it once when you next open the app.
