---
title: Why FITOS shows your trend weight, not today's number
date: 2026-10-20
description: Your scale can move a kilo overnight without you gaining or losing anything. Here's what FITOS shows instead, and why.
---

Step on a scale two mornings in a row and the number can jump by a kilo. You didn't gain a kilo of fat overnight — that's water, salt, food still being digested, and a dozen other things. Over a week, the noise can be bigger than the change you're actually making.

That's why the big number on the FITOS Progress screen is your **trend weight**, not your latest reading.

## What the trend is

The trend is a smoothed line through your readings: each new weigh-in nudges it, but no single morning can throw it around. The dots on the chart are your actual readings; the line is the trend. Your latest reading is still shown, smaller, underneath.

## What FITOS waits for

A trend needs data, so FITOS is honest about what it can't tell you yet:

- the **weekly rate** appears after about ten days of readings;
- the **change** appears once your readings span at least a week;
- FITOS never shows a day-to-day change at all.

## How to weigh in

A few times a week is plenty. Same time of day, ideally in the morning before you eat. Then stop looking at single days — the trend will tell you where you're going.
