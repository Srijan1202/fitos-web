Beta 3.6 is built from your feedback: three new things, and the fixes you asked for.

## What should I eat? (Premium)

On **Eat**, tap **Ask FITOS AI what to eat**:

- pick the meal, tick what you have (bread, eggs, curd, oats, banana…) or type it, and use today's mess menu if you like;
- say what you feel like, if you want: *something spicy*, *something light*.

FITOS AI suggests up to three plates. Each shows its calories and protein and how it fits what is left of your day. Tap **Log this** to log it.

- **The numbers are FITOS's own,** from the food database and the mess menu, never guessed by the AI.
- **Your diet and allergies are respected.** Anything FITOS doesn't know yet is listed rather than invented.

## Weekly steps

Tap **Steps** on Home to see:

- a bar for each day against your goal;
- your total, daily average, best day and days at goal;
- earlier weeks, with the arrows;
- **4 weeks**, each week's daily average.

Your steps come from Health Connect on your phone and never leave it.

## Feedback, with pictures and replies

In **Profile → Send feedback**:

- **Pictures:** add up to three screenshots or photos.
- **Phone details:** your phone's model and Android version go with the message (you can switch that off).
- **Replies:** when we reply, you get a notification. The reply shows under **Your messages**.

## Fixes

- **Camera photos are kept.** A progress photo taken with the camera now saves, and if your phone closed FITOS while the camera was open, FITOS offers to finish adding it.
- **"+ Add set" works first tap,** even right after scrolling.
- **Remove a set during a workout:** swipe it left, or tap **− Remove set**. There's Undo, and your programme stays as it is.
- **Progress photos stay hidden** until you tap **Show photos**, so nobody sees them over your shoulder.
- **Meal reminders** now open the meal they're about.
