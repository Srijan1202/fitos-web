Beta 3.4 adds two things you asked for: a reason to keep your streak going, and a better way to see your progress.

## Streak rewards

Your streak now unlocks rewards that only a streak can earn, the same for everyone:

| Streak | You unlock |
|---|---|
| 7 days | **Spark**, a profile picture |
| 14 days | **Ember**, a warm dark theme |
| 30 days | **Blaze**, an app icon |
| 60 days | **Ember rim** around your picture |
| 100 days | **Century**, a picture and a 100-day badge |

- **What counts:** they're earned by your best streak, and **your past streaks count too**. If you've already had a two-week run, Spark and Ember are waiting for you.
- **Yours to keep:** once earned, a reward stays yours, even if your streak breaks.
- **Where to find them:** see what you've earned, and how far the next one is, in **Profile → Streak rewards**.

## Progress photos

The mirror often shows progress before the scale does. **Progress → Photos**:

- take a photo or pick one from your gallery, and tag it front, side or back;
- your weight that day is saved with it;
- **Compare** puts two photos side by side, for example *30 days apart · −1.5 kg*, or drag a slider between them.

**Your photos never leave your phone.** They're not uploaded anywhere, they're left out of your phone's cloud backup, and they're deleted if you delete your account. That also means uninstalling FITOS removes them, so save any photo you want to keep to your gallery.
