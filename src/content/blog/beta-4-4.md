## Replay a run

Open any run and tap **Replay**. Your route draws itself on the map in about 20 seconds, with a dot showing where you were. Pause it, drag the slider to any moment, or play it at half or double speed. At every moment you can see your time, distance and pace.

## Where you were fast, and where you weren't

A run's map is now coloured by pace against your own average for that run: **green** where you were faster, **yellow** around your average, **red** where you slowed. A small legend under the map explains it.

## Plans with 1 or 2 runs a week

Every running plan now offers **1 to 5 runs a week**:

- **One run a week:** for a 5K or 10K it alternates a longer easy run and a faster session. For a half or a marathon it's your long run, building week by week.
- **Two runs a week:** a long run and a faster session.
- **The beginner run/walk 5K:** 1 to 3 days a week.

A half marathon or marathon on only 1 or 2 runs a week is possible, but much harder to finish well, and FITOS tells you so.

## Your sessions go with you

Sessions you build yourself are now saved to your account, not only your phone. A new phone, or a reinstall, keeps them. If you make one offline, it goes up when you're back online.

## Reminders that know it's a run day

On a plan run's day, your reminder now talks about the run, not the gym.

## Streak & rewards

Tap the **streak card** on Home to see your streak, your best, this week, and the last 4 weeks day by day: logged, rest day, skipped or missed. Every reward is listed below it, with when it unlocks.
