Beta 3.3 adds three things you asked for, plus one for us behind the scenes.

## A streak, and your week at a glance

Home now opens with a small card:

- **🔥 Your streak.** A day counts when you log a workout or anything you ate. Planned rest days and **Skip today** never break it. If today isn't logged yet, the card reminds you, gently.
- **The weekly ring.** The workouts you've done this week against your plan, for example **2/4**. Tap it to go to Training.

## Rate your mess food

Open a dish on your mess menu and give it a **👍 or 👎**. You can rate what was served today or in the last 7 days, change your vote, or tap it again to take it back. Once five people have voted, every dish shows how liked it is, for example **👍 82 %**. Votes are anonymous: nobody sees who voted, not even us.

## Share your programme

Found a split that works? **Training → ⋯ → Share programme** gives you a code like **FIT-K7Q2XM** and a link to send.

- Your friend opens the link to see the programme, or enters the code in **Training → ⋯ → Use a shared programme**. New users also find it under "How do you want to train?".
- They get the days, exercises, sets and reps. **Never your weights or your name.**
- Their own history and records stay theirs, and their old programme is kept.

## Behind the scenes

We can now switch a feature off from our side if something misbehaves, without making you install a new version.
