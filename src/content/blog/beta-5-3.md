FITOS has a new look: **Cobalt**. Black with cobalt-blue buttons when your phone is in dark mode, and the new **Cobalt Light** when it's in light mode.

## What changes when you update

- **Your theme starts again on Automatic.** FITOS opens in Cobalt or Cobalt Light, matching your phone. Your old theme is still there if you want it: Profile → Appearance.
- **A blue app icon.** The FITOS icon is now the blue F on black. Prefer the lime one? Pick **Classic** in Profile → Appearance. It's free.
- **The FITOS logo inside the app** turns blue with Cobalt.

## Two new Premium themes

- **Violet:** black with purple buttons and a purple glow.
- **Violet Light:** soft white with deep purple text and purple buttons.

Both come with Premium, next to Night, Forest and Ocean.

## A tidier Profile

**Appearance** is now folded away in Profile. It shows the theme and icon you're on; tap it to change either.

## The website too

tryfitos.me is in Cobalt as well, and follows your device's light or dark setting.
