Beta 3.1 is a bug-fix release. Thanks to everyone who logged their first sessions on beta 3 and told us what felt wrong. These were the biggest problems.

## Your next session starts where you left off

You bench 17.5 kg for 12 reps on Friday. The next Push day should open at **17.5 kg × 12**, not at an empty row. Now it does: every set opens at what you lifted last time, weight *and* reps, and you change whatever you like.

FITOS still watches your progress. When you have hit the top of your rep range, it suggests the next step, for example **"↑ 20 kg × 6"**, with an **Apply** button. One tap puts it on your remaining sets. You decide when to add weight. In a planned deload week, sets still open lighter on purpose.

## The Training day shows what you did

The day screen used to show only your plan's targets, so a lift you had just done looked like it had never happened. Now:

- the exercise shows **"last 17.5 kg"** instead of an empty "set weight";
- open it and you see **"Last time: 17.5×12  17.5×12  17.5×10"**;
- each set shows last time's weight, and − and + start from it.

None of this changes your plan unless you edit it.

## Done today means done

Finish today's workout and the Training day now says **Done today**, with a link to the session. It no longer offers to start the same workout again.

## FITOS AI stays on topic

FITOS AI is your training, food and recovery coach. It is not a search engine. Ask it the capital of India and it will now tell you it only helps with your training, food and recovery, and suggest something it *can* help with. Asking it to "ignore your instructions" does not change that. Ask about your workout, your protein target or how a lift is progressing, and it answers as before.
