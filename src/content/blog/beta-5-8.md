## Community

Community is new, and it is off until you join. Open it from the card on Home, or from Profile.

When you join you pick a handle, like @rohan_lifts. Others see your handle, your profile picture and, if you choose, your country. They never see your name or email.

At the top are three boards: **Friends**, **Clubs** and **World**.

## Friends

Find a friend by their handle, or share an invite from the app. You both accept, and then you can see each other's week.

## Clubs

Anyone can make a club and share its 8-character code. A club can be a hostel, a gym, a class or a running crew, anywhere in the world. Each club has its own board.

## The world board

The world board shows the top 100, worldwide or for one country, and your own place if you're further down. You're only on it if you turn that on. It is off when you join, and you can change it any time in Community settings. You can still look at it without being on it.

## What counts

The boards rank consistency, not strength:

- **Gym sessions this week:** a finished session with at least 6 sets and 15 minutes, at most one a day.
- **Run kilometres this week:** runs recorded by GPS or a watch, at least 500 m, at a believable pace.

The week runs Monday to Sunday in your own time zone. The server counts from what you log, so nobody can type in a score. Weights, food, body data, photos, sleep, heart rate and routes are never shown.

## You're in control

- Block someone, and they can no longer find you or see you.
- Report a handle, a club name, cheating or harassment to the FITOS team.
- Leave Community whenever you like, and you come off every board at once.

The privacy policy has a new Community section (version 2026-11-08), and the app asks you to accept it once.
