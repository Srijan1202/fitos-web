Beta 5.0 shows you how to do your lifts, and puts FITOS on your home screen.

## See how it's done

30 of the most-used lifts now have a demo. They cover squats, deadlifts and hip thrusts, lunges, bench and push-ups, presses, rows, pull-ups and pulldowns, curls and triceps, raises and flyes, leg machines, calf raises, the plank and hanging leg raises.

- **On the exercise's page,** above the steps. The figure is drawn in your theme's colours, with the bar, dumbbells, bench or cable it uses.
- **During a workout,** tap the small play icon beside an exercise's name. The demo and the steps open without leaving your session.
- **Numbered moments** match the written steps: tap 2 to see what step 2 looks like. Tap the demo to pause it, or tap **Slow** for half speed.
- It plays three times, then waits on the start, so it isn't moving while you read.
- With Android's **Remove animations** on, you step through still pictures instead.

Close variants share a demo, with a line saying what's different. A chin-up shows the pull-up with "Palms facing you". A list shows which lifts have a demo, marked with a small play icon.

The demos are free for everyone.

## FITOS on your home screen

Touch and hold your home screen, then go to **Widgets → FITOS**. There are four:

- **Food left:** a ring of what you've eaten against your target, with calories and protein left.
- **Today's training:** today's workout or planned run, or a rest day, with **Start**.
- **Streak:** your streak and this week's days, in the same colours as Streak & rewards.
- **Quick actions:** Log food, Workout, Run and Weight in one tap.

They follow your phone's dark or light mode, and they move to the new day at midnight even if you don't open FITOS. A tap opens the right screen; nothing starts until you tap there.

The widgets only show what's already on your phone, to anyone who sees your home screen. Remove a widget to hide it. Signed out, they show nothing.
