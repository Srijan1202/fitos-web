## Dark and Light, following your phone

FITOS has two new main themes:

- **Dark:** near-black with white buttons.
- **Light:** near-white with black buttons.

They're calm and simple, so your numbers stand out. Green still means good, amber means watch out, and red means something needs attention.

Out of the box, FITOS now follows your phone. In dark mode you get **Dark**, and in light mode you get **Light**. Switch your phone's mode and FITOS switches with it.

## Cobalt

**Cobalt** is a new theme for everyone: true black with blue buttons.

## Your choice, still

Go to **Profile → Theme**. **Automatic** comes first and follows your phone. Pick any other theme to keep it whatever your phone does:

- Dark, Light, Cobalt;
- the green FITOS Dark and FITOS Light;
- Night, Forest and Ocean with Premium;
- the themes you earned.

After this update everyone starts on Automatic, so if you liked the green FITOS Dark, pick it again in Profile.
