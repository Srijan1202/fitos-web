Beta 4.9 adds race day, more from your Galaxy Watch, and more food. It also fixes three things you told us about.

## Race day

Pick **Race** when you start a run. Choose 5K, 10K, half or marathon, type your target time, and choose **Even** or **Negative** splits. With negative splits you run the first half a little slower (2 %) and the second half a little faster, for the same finish time. FITOS shows the pace plan before you start.

During the race you see:
- the pace to hold right now, in big numbers;
- how many seconds you are ahead of the plan (green) or behind it (red);
- the finish time your pace so far gives.

The voice coach says where you stand at every kilometre.

Afterwards, the run's page shows your time against the target ("32 s under target") and each kilometre against the plan. The share picture shows it too. A race in your running plan starts in race mode on its own.

## Cadence from your watch

Watch runs now show your average and maximum **cadence**, in steps a minute, next to your heart rate.

## Watch runs arrive on their own

Your Galaxy Watch runs used to arrive only when you opened Running. Now FITOS checks for new ones every few hours, even when the app is closed. You need the Galaxy Watch switch on in Running settings, and background access allowed in Health Connect. To allow it, turn the switch off and on again. When a run arrives you get one quiet notification, at most once a day.

The privacy policy now says this, so FITOS will ask you to accept it once.

## 25 more dishes

Lasagne, savoury and sweet pies, soufflés, mousse, handvo and more from the Indian food database. A whole cake comes as a slice (an eighth), a pie as a sixth, and a dish as a quarter.

## Fixes

- **Rest days are green.** Any day you kept your streak without training is a rest day. Blue means you trained. The legend reads Trained · Rest · Skipped · Missed.
- **Running badges are medals** with their number on them ("50 km", "5K 30′"), in their group's colour.
- **The weight graph follows your weigh-ins.** A bold line goes through your actual readings, with a dot on each. The trend is a thin dashed line behind it.

## Getting it

This one is a full update, because of the new background permission. Open FITOS and tap **Update now**, or [download beta 4.9](/download/).
