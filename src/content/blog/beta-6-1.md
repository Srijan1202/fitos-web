## Train in cycles, not weekdays

Training ⋯ → **Schedule** now has **Days on / off**. Pick 4 on 1 off, 3 on 1 off or your own numbers, and your plan becomes a cycle with no weekdays: Day 1, Day 2 and so on, starting today. **Skip today** moves the whole cycle a day. You can switch back to weekdays at any time, and your history stays.

## Build with FITOS AI (Premium)

Tell FITOS AI how often you can train, for how long, and what you want to bring up. You can leave out exercises you don't want. It builds a week from the exercises your equipment allows. FITOS then checks it: each session fits your time and no muscle gets too many sets. You see anything it changed before you use the plan.

## A week of mess meals (Premium)

Mess → **This week** shows a plate for every meal of the next seven days, planned by FITOS from the menu, your diet and your targets. Days the mess hasn't published yet are marked. You can log today's plates with one tap.

## Monthly report (Premium)

Progress → **Monthly report (PDF)** puts your month on one page: your sessions, your hardest-worked muscles, your records, runs, food, water, sleep and weight trend. FITOS makes the PDF on your phone and never uploads it. Share it or save it wherever you like.

## And

- **Lift history:** tap a lift in Progress → Strength for every session you've done it, with your estimated 1RM over time.
- The evening protein reminder can now name tonight's mess dish with the most protein that suits your diet.
- Your theme follows your account to any phone you sign in on.
- Premium members can get some features before everyone else (early access).

## Privacy

The privacy policy (version 2026-11-10) now covers three things:

- What **Build with FITOS AI** sends to Google's Gemini: your answers, your goal and experience, and the exercises you can do. It never sends your name, weight or history.
- Your theme choice, which is now kept with your account.
- The monthly PDF, which is made on your phone.

The app asks you to accept the new policy once.

## Getting it

This is a new version, 1.0.0-beta.6.1, and you need it to keep using FITOS. The app will ask you to update, or you can download it from this site. It installs over the version you have and keeps everything.
