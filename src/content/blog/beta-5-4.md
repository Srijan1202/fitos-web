FITOS AI used to know your plan and your workouts. Now it knows more of your day, so you can ask things like **"What should I eat tonight?"** or **"How much protein is left today?"**.

## What FITOS AI sees now

- **Today's food:** what you've logged today and what's left of your targets. Earlier days are never sent.
- **Your check-in:** today's readiness and how you've slept and felt over the last week.
- **Your weight trend:** the trend line, not your individual weigh-ins.
- **Your mess:** today's menu, and whether the menu was published or worked out from the weekly cycle.

## When the AI is busy

The free AI is sometimes busy. FITOS now tries again for you, and then asks a lighter model. While it does, you'll see **"FITOS AI is busy — trying again…"** instead of an error.

## Privacy

The privacy policy now lists exactly what chat sends. FITOS asks you to agree once more the next time you open it. You can turn FITOS AI off any time in Profile → Privacy.

## Getting it

The update comes to the app by itself. Close FITOS and open it again; there's nothing to download.
